# 09: Generate evidence-linked manager findings

**What to build:** Have the manager inspect public evidence, test competing explanations and propose structured findings, causal update reasons and scoped operator recommendations.

**Blocked by:** 06: Show scoped local findings and coverage; 08: Expose trustworthy consistency and uncertainty limits.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Manager tools are read-only and pinned to an immutable snapshot with revision-labelled results; no tool can inspect truth, dispatch drones, overwrite observations or release a hold.
- [ ] Hypotheses declare falsifiable claims, spatial scope, predictions, required conditions, qualitative assessment and manager-owned urgency distinct from probability.
- [ ] Electrical candidates are tested in isolation against the pinned observations and assumptions before the final explanation; indistinguishable or unsupported candidates remain explicit.
- [ ] Every proposed creation, assessment, scope, prediction, priority or lifecycle change includes full before/after values, finding_summary, update_reason and remaining uncertainty.
- [ ] Measurement-triggered revisions cite actual trigger events and every latest tied measurement, including values, units, location, conditions, contrary evidence and referenced diagnostics.
- [ ] Startup cites reports and states that no drone measurements exist; operator-triggered changes identify their real event. Unchanged hypotheses receive a turn assessment without a semantic revision.
- [ ] Structured validation rejects missing explanations, invalid evidence links and unsupported numerical claims. The console displays clearly proposed findings and scoped recommendations.
- [ ] Scripted manager outputs verify the proposal contract, while a recorded live turn demonstrates LLM-authored findings and reasons.
