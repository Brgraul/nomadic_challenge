# 12: Value shared evidence without multiplying rewards

**What to build:** Explain and compare plans by unique diagnostic benefit under manager-owned urgency, so overlapping tasks or differently worded hypotheses cannot inflate a plan's value.

**Blocked by:** 11: Generate tasks only for accepted diagnostic needs.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Eligibility is checked before scoring; rewards are restricted to active diagnostic needs or explicit benchmark reconstruction targets.
- [ ] Shared evidence has one benefit group owned by the highest accepted associated urgency; the planner cannot change urgency to force an assignment.
- [ ] Local coverage utility uses the fixed 10 m grid and conserved length-based points; overlapping scopes and cell subdivision cannot increase total reward.
- [ ] Confirmation utility is awarded once per justified group; repeat electrical readings earn no artificial structural-rank benefit.
- [ ] Electrical benefit uses joint conditional variance reduction with full correlations, conditioned in urgency order, and the specified fixed normalization for the decision.
- [ ] One fixed joint scorer compares candidates and incumbents across surrogate rounds, recording integer quantization tolerance and unavailable utility.
- [ ] Strict urgency tiers precede risk, switching and airtime; previews show score assumptions and synthetic units without claiming calibrated causal entropy.
- [ ] Tests cover shared hypotheses, overlapping footprints, subdivision, correlated repeats and higher-urgency benefit competing with many lower-urgency tasks.
