Grid Ops: evidence-driven outage investigation

After a storm, we diagnose why the power went out. Five drones gather measurements, three analysis engines turn them into evidence, two LLM agents form and test root-cause hypotheses, and an optimizer re-routes the fleet every time something new is learned. The loop stops once the cause is supported.

1 · Inputs

Storm reportsSix incomplete, possibly misleading hints on where to start
Grid map & topologyReal Bavarian overhead lines, substations, circuits
Operator consoleManual dispatch, scenario choice, approvals
seed hypotheses + network model

2 · Shared evidence (single source of truth)

Drone readingsVoltage / current / power, line clearance, temperature, conductor motion
Charging observationsWhich lines are live (a drone can only recharge on an energized line)
Public world stateImmutable snapshots; one writer; hidden truth never enters
every new reading triggers re-analysis

3 · Diagnostic engines (deterministic)

Power-flow estimationReduced DC model fitted to readings; reports what is and isn't observable
Consistency checksDo readings fit the assumed topology? Flags bad data and unsupported claims
Hazard & coverageVegetation, sag, heat, wind near each line; what has been inspected vs. still unknown
diagnostics + uncertainty

4 · Reasoning (LLM agents, async)

ManagerRanks root-cause hypotheses, explains every revision with evidence, recommends the operator action, decides when to stop
PlannerTurns open questions into measurement needs: what to measure, where, and how it could change the diagnosis
measurement needs + candidate sites

5 · Route optimization

Multi-drone route solverCP-SAT; picks stops per drone to maximize unique evidence, reroutes airborne drones in <1 s
Route validatorBattery, dwell time, deadline, and a guaranteed recharge landing on a live line
validated routes (or safe landing fallback)

6 · Fleet execution

5 dronesFly to points on the lines, measure, land on live circuits to recharge
Simulation clockAdvances time, applies state changes, publishes new readings
new readings → back to layer 2

Output

Root cause + recommended actionEvidence-linked, with remaining uncertainty stated
Live map & explanation logHypotheses, readings, coverage and routes for the operator
↻ Closed loop
Layers 2 → 6 repeat on every reading until the cause is supported

Hidden synthetic grid

A full AC power-flow simulator (pandapower) plays "reality". Our system only ever sees its sensor readings, never the answer.

Isolated evaluation

Scores the diagnosis against the hidden truth. Never feeds back into the agents.

Why not just reconstruct all flows?

Measure only what could change the diagnosis. Full flow reconstruction is a separate benchmark mode.
